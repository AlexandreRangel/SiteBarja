Sobre a obra

CULTURA X NATUREZA = BAR- BÁRIE - O animal é alheio por excelência, o outro realmente incompreensível. A equação parece concluída: “Cultura X Natureza = Barbárie” (1997 -2004). Ao final, dois elementos incompatíveis, provocam a destruição. De certa forma o artista parece colocar, diante de nossos olhos, o negativo do sonho romântico que representava Beuys, sua confiança na possibilidade de utilizar a energia dos animais por alcançar a unidade desejada que já, muito antes, havia anunciado, por exemplo, Schiller quando invejava a proximidade dos gregos com a natureza: “são o que nós fomos; são o que devemos voltar a ser. Temos sido natureza como eles, e nossa cultura deve retornar pelo caminho da razão e da liberdade à natureza”. Mercedes Replinger   González extraído do livro Instalações Barja - 2005; do texto“Documentos incertos no lugar do monstro”. CULTURE X NATURE = BARBA- RISM - Animals are foreign par excellence, the truly incomprehensible other. The equation looks complete: “Culture versus Nature = Barbarism” (1997-2004). At the end, two incompatible elements provoke destruction. In a way, the artist seems to place before our eyes the negative of the romantic dream that Beuys represented, his confidence in the possibility of utilizing animal energy to arrive at that desired state of unity that Schiller, for example, had already exalted much earlier in expressing his envy of the Greeks’ proximity to nature: “they are that which we were, that which we must return to being. Like them, we have been nature, and our culture must follow the path of reason and freedom back to nature.” Mercedez Replinger González extract from Instalações Barja - 2005 / “Uncertain documents in place of the monster”

… Barja’s poetics fall outside the realm of visual poetry, we should place much of his works into its expanded visual coordinates. This is especially true when the nomenclature forms part of the work, and the naming has a determinant function in a triangle of verbal, conceptual and visual powers. Many of these works, in fact, start working when this verbal-visual element is activated, generating a multiple aesthetic force field where even the sensorial territory is produced by conceptual and reflexive contiguity. It’s in this sense that Da², H2O- Monet, Fike and even Natureza X Cultura = Barbárie stand concerted. Present in each is the submerged conversation that establishes itself and reaches for a certain infinitude, in a writing (“this insane game of writing”, as Blanchot would say) that, here, also compete for a simultaneity of languages. The very idea of “Obranome”, (curatorial and work motif) operates on this non-reductionist frequency. The current of thought Barja transmits is unwilling to loll in retinal anesthesia.
