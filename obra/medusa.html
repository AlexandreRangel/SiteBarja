Na sua segunda versão, articulada com as obras para Hermógenes, essa experiência era vista “de fora” protegida por um véu (como re-visão da primeira e como antevisão da outra subversão, a da arte visual para quem não vê). Bengalas-pincel e bengalas-signos, objetos submersos, escrituras invisíveis, a Agulha-no-Palheiro (cujo subtítulo é “para nivelar visibilidades”) transmitida por uma tv-no-palheiro, a bacia-instrumento sonoro “ Sol de Goethe”, a Medusa, com suas bengalas/lâminas que furam a íris de um olho, a reafirmar por outra via a maldição do mito, ou o enorme cobertor negro, com inscrições em braile bordadas com pérolas, seu Livro de Barriga. The artist resists hiding wounds and does not recoil from writing the story found in the legible footprint that is the blind man’s memory. As he himself related to blind photographer and thinker Evgen Bavcar, a man with developed listening skills, “that day in Rio seemed to have come about by chance, as I listened to the whiteness and darkness of your words recounting the fate of your grandfather, stricken by the gaze of the Francoist Medusa. The white cane thus became an invisible canvas, a painting to be created, while your grandfather’s wounded body became the shadow of a gaze seeking refuge in a concrete pictorial utopia”(14). A lugubrious memory, interwoven at the same time with another memory of the spoken accounts, mostly stories and successes drawn from newspapers and television dramas, delivered by the young boy to his grandfather, constructing an imaginary world out of words.
